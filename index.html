01 / About

Curiosity at the intersection
of code and intelligence.

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Mustapha Stambouli, Mascara, Algeria. My work brings together software engineering, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity. I head the AIIS research team at LABTEC-IA, with a focus on turning research ideas into useful methods, tools, and shared datasets.

My research began with a question: how can we find vulnerable code earlier and more precisely? Today, I explore that question through code metrics, natural language processing, and deep learning—and extend these ideas to software evolution and co-change recommendation.
